# C2 implementation and experimental boundary (prepared before C1 finishes)

Status: core implementation prepared, **simulation efficacy not tested**. No change to V28, the frozen generator, tracker, original goals, physical thresholds or progression gate.

The mechanism is repeat-agreement feedback plus componentwise quality guarding. Every input has exactly three construction/execution calls: the initial native-prefix candidate, an identical-condition/identical-noise repeat, and one bounded correction. The repeat is charged, leaving one update, not two. Comparison arms also receive three calls. Independent selected-reference replay is charged identically across arms. The earlier idea of shrinking a later update is not tested under this three-call budget and must not be claimed.

For completed repetitions, position feedback agrees when its two error vectors differ by at most 5% of the unchanged 10cm goal tolerance; signed heading feedback agrees within 5% of the unchanged heading tolerance. These are method hyperparameters, not relaxed success thresholds. Failed/incomplete repeats do not license an update. In the no-agreement ablation the repeated call is still paid, but the first valid endpoint error is used directly. Corrected model conditions remain bounded by the original C1 step/total limits; external evaluation targets and event times remain fixed.

The quality guard retains the incumbent unless a complete candidate has finite values and does not worsen any condition error, calibrated surface-slip proxy, penetration fraction, jerk or tracking error. A tie keeps the incumbent. A complete construction may replace an incomplete construction, but the failed attempt is retained. Joint success still uses the same frozen predicates and source denominator; no source is discarded and no candidate is labelled successful just because it was accepted.

Required controls: three-call random/native best, C1 with its two updates, repeated-call/no-agreement, no-quality-guard, and within-family source-shuffled agreement labels. Use the preselected source splits, goals and three seeds; preserve all failed and rejected attempts. Native deterministic repeats may agree exactly, making the agreement ablation indistinguishable. Such a result is a negative result for the added agreement mechanism, not a reason to inject noise or claim reliability improvement. Agreement cannot detect systematic bias or prove feasibility under a different controller.

Simulation launcher and fresh documented runtime witness are still required before running this core in a full experiment. C1 results must first establish whether the joint-quality endpoint discriminates the methods. Neither this code nor a numeric check is a validated innovation; closest prior quality-gating/trust mechanisms must be compared separately.
