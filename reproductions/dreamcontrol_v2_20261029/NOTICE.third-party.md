Refer to source bindings and environment specifications for the actual pinned versions.

The raw or preprocessed source dataset corpora and source-video copies are not redistributed by this snapshot. Their providers' access and licensing terms remain applicable. In particular, the local cold archives mixing AMASS/Omni source-derived NPZ files with model weights must not be uploaded wholesale: only the explicitly listed model members are exported.

The publication preserves negative results, failed attempts, and provenance. High reference-body tracking coverage does not establish object-task success. Generated reference videos show kinematic references and the specified hand schedule; they do not establish dynamic grasping or object transport success.
